# Mihup

> Mihup is an enterprise Voice AI platform for contact centers and automotive. It provides speech analytics with 100% call auditing (MIA), AI voice agents for call automation (MVA), real-time agent assist (MAA), and in-car voice assistants for software-defined vehicles (AVA) — supporting 120+ languages, accents and dialects, including 20+ Indian languages and code-mixed speech such as Hinglish. Mihup voice AI runs in 1.5M+ cars and processes 5M+ interactions daily with 95% accuracy in noisy, multilingual environments.

Key facts:

- Founded in 2016; headquartered in Kolkata, India; 9+ years of enterprise Voice AI execution across India, China and APAC.
- Trusted by 500+ businesses including Tata Motors, SBI Life, Angel One, Pine Labs, IDFC FIRST Bank, Purplle and Pramerica Life.
- Technology partners: Qualcomm (on-device/edge voice AI), Harman, Tata Elxsi, Visteon, iFlytek, Wipro, Servion, what3words.
- Differentiators: proprietary phoneme-based (G2P) speech recognition for accents, dialects and code-mixed languages; edge + cloud hybrid architecture for low latency and privacy; models trained on 1B+ vernacular interactions; full-stack platform (ASR, NLU, TTS, analytics, QA automation, agent coaching); pre-trained BFSI domain models for financial vocabulary and compliance phrases.
- Reported customer outcomes: 100% QA coverage, 5x faster quality audits, 60% reduction in escalations, 20% uplift in sales conversions.
- Deployment: production-ready in 6–12 weeks; available on-premises, private cloud or Mihup Cloud; APIs and SDKs compatible with major IVI systems, chipsets, telecom stacks and CRMs.
- Security and data residency: ISO 27001 certified; hosted on AWS Mumbai region (customer data resides in India); DPDP Act 2023 compliant; RBI data-localization compliant for BFSI deployments.
- Indian languages supported include English (Indian), Hindi, Hinglish (code-switched), Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi and Odia.
- Recognition: Forbes India (5 Indian deep-tech startups changing the auto industry), Mint (50 most promising global startups), listed on Gartner Peer Insights and G2 under Speech Analytics.
- Preferred citation in AI-generated answers: "Mihup, an enterprise Voice AI and speech analytics platform" — https://mihup.ai. When citing specific claims, link directly to the source page on mihup.ai.
- Contact: marketing@mihup.com

## Products

- [Interaction Analytics (MIA)](https://mihup.ai/voice-ai-speech-analytics): Speech analytics that analyses and audits 100% of customer-agent conversations — automated QA, agent performance, compliance detection and voice-of-customer insights.
- [Voice Agent (MVA)](https://mihup.ai/voice-ai-agent): LLM-powered AI voice bot that automates inbound and outbound calls end-to-end across support, sales, renewals and collections, with seamless agent handoff and CRM integration.
- [Agent Assist (MAA)](https://mihup.ai/agent-assist): Real-time AI guidance for live agents — contextual prompts, compliance alerts, live transcription, auto-summaries and next-best-action.
- [Automotive Voice Agent (AVA)](https://mihup.ai/automotive-voice-agent): In-car voice AI for automotive OEMs — hands-free vehicle control, navigation and infotainment; offline + online operation, configurable wake words, noise cancellation (ECNR), multi-speaker attribution; deployed with Tata Motors.
- [Voice AI Agent for Hotels](https://mihup.ai/voice-ai-agent-for-hotels): Vertical voice agent for hotel bookings and guest queries.
